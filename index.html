Industrial Assessment

Let’s understand your factory before we recommend a solution.

Before you start

Four short steps, about five minutes. Having these ready helps:

  • Approximate daily production in kg
  • Number of processing machines and shifts
  • Any meters or monitoring you already use
  • The one problem that costs you most

What happens next

  1. We read your request and review your factory profile.
  2. We contact you by your preferred method.
  3. We hold an assessment call to understand your processes before recommending anything.

Do not send confidential recipes or formulas. TPRIS never changes process chemistry.

FAQ

Straight answers for factory owners.

Is the data on this website real?

No. Every figure here is simulated for demonstration. No factory is connected and no customer results are claimed.

Do I need new meters or sensors?

Machine-level electricity, water, steam and effluent data need submeters or instruments. Where meters do not exist, batch and downtime records can be entered manually to start.

Can TPRIS work with our existing systems?

Integration with existing ERP, PLC or SCADA systems is possible and is scoped after we understand your setup. Nothing is connected by default.

Will it tell us to change our dye recipes?

No. TPRIS records and compares chemical usage. Any recipe or process change must be approved by qualified textile-processing personnel.

Can you guarantee savings or regulatory compliance?

No. The ROI calculator shows hypothetical estimates from your own assumptions. TPRIS supports compliance reporting but does not certify compliance.

Do you use AI?

Today it is transparent, rule-based detection against baselines built from your own batches. Advanced analytics will be offered only after validation on real factory data.

What does the first assessment involve?

A conversation about your processes, resource usage and problems. We recommend a solution only after that.
